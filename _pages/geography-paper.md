<p> We used satellite images over the Northern Canadian Arctic to analyze trends in the first day of melt of sea ice over the last 10 and 40 years. Most importantly, we focused on developing high-resolution maps of the area, attributable to Sentinel-1's enhanced spatial resolution compared to previous satellites. Changes to the first day of melt in this region affect the surface energy budget, and melt onset trends can provide insight into melt trends in other regions of the Arctic.</p>
